# Apple iPhone Duo product bezels

Source: https://developer.apple.com/design/resources/#product-bezels
Download: https://devimages-cdn.apple.com/design/resources/download/Bezel-iPhone-Duo.dmg

- `duo-inner-landscape.png`: iPhone Duo - Night Sky - Inner Open Landscape.png (3093 × 2247); screen aperture 2853 × 2007 at (120, 120).
- `duo-outer-portrait.png`: iPhone Duo - Night Sky - Outer Closed Portrait.png (1574 × 2194); screen aperture 1398 × 2032 at (88, 81).

Both PNGs are byte-for-byte copies of Apple's supplied files. They are Apple design resources, NOT licensed under this project's AGPL. The accompanying Apple Design Resources License applies; consult it and Apple's marketing guidelines before reuse. Do not treat these assets as freely redistributable source art for unrelated projects.

The PNG files themselves are unmodified. The existing hero positioning and layered composition are retained at the project owner’s request; check Apple’s marketing guidelines before using that composition in release marketing. Other screenshot panels use upright product images without added product shadows. Screenshot content sits behind the PNG in the matching screen aperture. Portrait screenshots have approximately the outer-display aspect ratio; landscape hero content matches the unfolded inner display.
